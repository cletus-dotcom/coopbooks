Annual Reporting Reminder

Cooperatives must submit CAPR with audited financial statements to CDA within 120 days after fiscal year-end (typically by April 30). Reports include Annex A (Statement of Financial Condition), Annex B (Statement of Operations), and Annex E (Statement of Cash Flows).
